How a project
actually runs.

Six stages, in order — each closes with a decision, not a deck.

  1. 01

    Consult & scope

    Walk the concept, budget, and timeline — leave with a written scope of which services apply.

  2. 02

    Form the company

    Entity, registration, and licensing filed before any lease is signed or dollar committed.

    Plan the business

    Projections and staffing sized to the site — the numbers a lender will actually check.

    Build & open

    Permits pulled, contractors managed, punch list closed — on site through the final walkthrough.
